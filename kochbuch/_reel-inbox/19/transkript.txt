You know when you save a recipe and swear you'll make it and then never do? Well please don't do that with this honey cashew chicken. You need to try it. It is quick, easy and high in protein and a huge star is the chicken itself. It becomes so incredibly soft and tender because we coat it in corn flour which locks in moisture and gives you that silky juicy texture. Honestly such a good hack to remember any time you're cooking chicken breast and want to avoid dryness. Everything else gets done in the wok in no time at all. First the cashews to toast then the chicken which will pull out so it doesn't overcook. Then the veggies and aromatics followed by that delicious super easy honey soy glaze. Serve it up with your favorite rice and you have got the perfect high protein takeaway at home situation made for a fraction of the price. Enjoy.
