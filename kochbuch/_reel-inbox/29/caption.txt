Easy 20 minute Cajun Chicken Alfredo 

This is a family favorite and our go to for a busy night!

You can find the full detailed recipe on my website, link in bio.

Recipe 
• Season chicken: 2 tbsp each: smoked paprika, garlic & onion powder, 1 tsp each salt & pepper, 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper, 1/2 tbsp garlic herb blend
• Cook chicken in skillet and flip 3 mins per side until IT reached 165F. Set aside.
• Add in 2 tbsp butter and 3 cloves of minced garlic to the pan
• Add in 1 cup chicken broth, 1 cup heavy whipping cream and 1 tbsp corn starch, 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
• Cooked fettuccine noodles then add to pan and thickened sauce
• Top with sliced chicken and fresh parsley
