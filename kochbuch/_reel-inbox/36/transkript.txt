This is how you make sesame ground chicken with sticky green beans. It comes together in under 20 minutes and uses just one pan. To make it, all you have to do is fry your ground chicken with red onion, fresh garlic and Chinese five spice. Once it's browned, add in sesame oil, soy sauce, rice wine, vinegar and honey. Fry until caramelized and stir through spring onion and fresh chili. Set the sesame ground chicken aside and then add green beans into the same pan. Fry them on high heat for a few minutes until they're slightly flisted and tender. Then add in sesame seeds and honey and fry for a few more minutes. Serve alongside rice and you're good to go.
