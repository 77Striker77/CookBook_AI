Kinder Cheesecake Pots 🎄🍫 ✨

Getting your ideas ready early for Christmas? These Quick & Easy Kinder Cheesecake Pots are the perfect make-ahead dessert — they take less than 20 minutes to prep and look so festive on the table! 🥰

For the base:
Blitz 200g digestives and mix with 120g melted butter.
Spoon into your pots or jars and press down firmly.

For the filling:
In a bowl, whisk together:
	•	360g cream cheese (room temp)
	•	1–2 tsp vanilla extract
	•	120g icing sugar

In a separate bowl, whisk 200ml double cream until thick, then fold it into the cream cheese mix.
Melt 50g Kinder chocolate and stir that through too.

Spoon the mixture onto your bases.

For the topping:
Microwave 150g chocolate with 150ml double cream for 1 minute.
Stir until smooth and glossy, then pour over each pot.

Chill overnight — and that’s it!
Creamy, chocolatey, and Christmas-ready in no time 🎅🍫✨
