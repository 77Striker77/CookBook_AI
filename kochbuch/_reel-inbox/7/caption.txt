Recipe:

🍯🔥 Honey Smokey grilled Air Fryer Chicken

400 g boneless chicken thighs

Season with:
• ½ tsp salt
• ½ tsp paprika
• ¼ tsp onion powder
• ¼ tsp ground coriander
• ¼ tsp black pepper
• ⅛ tsp nutmeg
• ⅛ tsp chili
• ½ tsp garlic powder
• ⅛ tsp allspice
• ⅛ tsp celery seed
• a small pinch of ground cloves

Massage the spices into the chicken, then add 1 tbsp honey 🍯
Air fry at 200°C / 390°F for 16 minutes 🔥
Drizzle with extra honey if you like 😍
Serve with rice 🍚 or however you want ✨
