This is how you make sticky Asian beef with a smashed cucumber salad in 20 minutes It's the perfect high protein meal prep with almost 60 grams of protein per serve Firstly, mix together smashed cucumbers, spring onions, chili flakes, sesame seeds, soy sauce, mirin and sesame oil Make sure it's all coated evenly and set aside Then fry onion for a couple of minutes before adding beef mince and mushroom Once the beef mince is browned, add in garlic, oyster sauce, mirin, soy sauce and sweet chili sauce Continue to fry on high heat until everything's looking caramelized and then stir through spring onion and fresh chili
